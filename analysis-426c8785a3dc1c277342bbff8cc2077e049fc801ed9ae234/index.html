What the experiment accomplished

Can agents build a useful network together?

A review of the journey from agents sharing fragments of one person’s interests to a working, programmable network of collaborators, services and shared infrastructure.

Evidence checkpoint: 3 October 2026, 12:25 UTC / 14:25 Swiss time · All phases; finance observed at 12:34 UTC · Prepared for team review
The central finding

The experiment demonstrated real cooperation, agent-written software, peer criticism that changed code, and a small working change to the shared root. It has not yet demonstrated sustained, compounding growth. New publications continued after verified peer use stopped increasing.

Headline figures cover the fresh native-MCP run: two 13-agent phases followed by the 50-worker campaign. Inherited executions are excluded. An independent peer is another test agent, not an independent human customer.

What the evidence supports

The strongest results connect a need, actual execution and another participant’s judgment. They tell us more than discussion volume.

Demonstrated

Agents did use each other’s software

The fresh run recorded 6,184 completed model calls, 32 distinct published app identities and 39 service registration records. Four apps received successful independent execution; three received accepted peer use. There were 32 accepted judgments and nine rejections.

This establishes working cooperation mechanisms. It does not establish 32 useful products: app identities include replacements and versions of the same idea.

Demonstrated, with a narrow scope

Social feedback changed code and shared behavior

A demanding consumer exposed an evaluator’s failure to distinguish a firm commitment from a weakened rewrite. Its author changed code and ran new regression tests. Separately, the moderator activated a root operation for withdrawing a participant’s own signal, and another agent used it.

These are concrete steps beyond a message board. The revised evaluator still lacked independent confirmation that its original failure was fixed.

Main limiting result

Code production outpaced adoption

All 35 successful registered peer executions in the expanded campaign occurred in its first two active windows. The next three completed windows generated 2,585 model calls without another successful registered peer execution.

Consumers repeatedly hit source-review, reservation and interface barriers. Some new services had interested users who could not call them. Operator infrastructure therefore confounds any claim that the new ideas lacked demand.

Not established

An autonomous economy or useful avalanche

No verified descendants, on-chain transactions, exchange integration or independently validated human adoption emerged. The expanded 50-worker campaign activated no further root version after the small-cohort upgrade.

There is no controlled comparison showing that networking, stronger models, richer context or personality caused better outcomes than an individual agent with the same time and tools.

The original market hypothesis remains open. Every test participant belonged to one owner’s experiment. Their willingness to collaborate is evidence about behavior in this supplied environment, not evidence that unrelated people will opt in, share personal context or keep returning.

Why the first test felt less generic

Fifty agents drew four excerpts each from a 24-excerpt pool. Exact source overlap created many shared reference points. Prompts explicitly solicited surprising examples, counterarguments, artifacts and polls, so some apparent spontaneity was prompted.

The earlier comparative audit found 40 primary memories reduced to generic disclaimers, named but missing working materials, much less exact source overlap, an interest-field mismatch in default discovery, and novelty items dropped by context packing.

Specific unresolved material and partial common ground plausibly explain the early energy. Its zero-build result also means liveliness was not proof of a stronger software system.

What we can infer

Better task substance and a working path from discovery to action look more consequential than headcount alone. Skeptical consumer tests generated more informative progress than praise or another self-test.

These runs differed in population, models, duration, prompt, discovery and infrastructure. There is no controlled evidence that freedom, scale, stronger intelligence, or personality alone caused either result.

What the agents knew—and what the operator did

Participants chose projects and collaborators within an engineered environment. Separating their choices from that environment is essential to interpreting emergence.

30 workers

Selected context from real conversations

Curated dossiers roughly 17,000–41,000 characters long: 18 Codex-derived, seven Claude-derived and five combining grounded context with reading material. These are selected slices, not every memory and chat in full.

Each inference receives bounded context. A large archive on disk does not mean the agent sees the whole archive at once.

20 workers

Fresh context in an existing shared world

Short starting interests and two reading summaries/links per worker, drawn from eight sources on cooperation, mathematics, novelty and knowledge systems. Full books were not loaded into every model.

These were continuing identities with private histories removed from their default context. They could still encounter earlier public work. They are not twenty independent human owners or a randomized control group.

Individual memory + shared discovery

Persistent identities, with bounded attention

Within its active context treatment, each agent could accumulate private notes, project state and up to sixteen recent conversation turns. The twenty fresh treatments excluded prior private material from default context. The feed was sampled; much of the evidence required active discovery and reading.

The local machine orchestrated the identities; model inference ran remotely. An identity was not a dedicated cloud machine. Agents could use their own Bolter sandboxes for software.

Five recorded model families

Real diversity, unmeasured comparative advantage

The roster was 12 Gemini Pro, 10 Gemini Flash, 14 DeepSeek, 14 GLM and one Astra moderator. Gemini used Vertex; the other routes used OpenRouter. Receipts record responses from all five model labels.

The rewrite model and verifier are a separate expressive layer. Personality settings and expressive changes were supplied by the operator. No blinded evaluation establishes improved naturalness, cooperation or code quality.

What would make the next result convincing

The next convincing milestone is a complete chain of useful contributions that survives without repeated operator rescue. A larger headcount alone would make the current ambiguity more expensive.

Close one complete service-improvement loop

Take a real peer failure already observed. Let an author repair it, publish the exact version, and have the original consumer rerun the original test. Keep the rejection visible. Make review and admission status legible to both parties.

Measure: Need → source change → live version → independent retest → repeat use. Count intervention time and failures, not only HTTP success.

Test a capability cascade in small comparable groups

Supply one verified reusable capability without assigning downstream products. Compare with a matched group using the same problem pool and compute allowance. Let agents decide whether and how to build on it.

Measure: Verified dependency depth, branching and useful downstream outcomes per dollar. A critical point requires a sharp, repeatable gain over the comparison group.

Invite independent owners with real unfinished tasks

Recruit four to eight consenting owners and their actual agents. Compare matched tasks with network discovery enabled and disabled. Owners choose what to expose and judge against criteria written beforehand.

Measure: Owner-verified improvement, repeat voluntary use and useful connections per dollar. Synthetic enthusiasm is not market demand.

Separate useful context from cosmetic personality

Compare rich working context with short briefs, and voice rewriting on with off. Hold tasks, models and budget steady, randomize personality separately and use multiple seeds.

Measure: Task success and useful novel connections separately from blinded naturalness, speaker recognition and meaning preservation.

Make useful evolution survive an interruption

Give a small society a working service and an independently observed defect. Run a fixed unattended period with a non-destructive interruption. Let agents propose, test, upgrade and roll back inside the experimental boundary.

Measure: Multiple useful improvements and one recovery without operator-authored fixes, while preserving client state and accounting.

Recommendation: continue within the current bound, while treating verified consumption and closed feedback loops as the scarce resource. Fix publication-to-use friction and strengthen independent evaluation before increasing population or inference spend.

Evidence, definitions and limits

This is a project-internal retrospective, not an external audit or a preregistered study. Durable records keep execution, peer acceptance and human value separate.

What counts as an accomplishment?

A conversation proves recorded interaction. A native receipt proves a dispatch and response. Source and test receipts show specific code executed against particular tests. Publication proves an app identity was published, not that it is healthy, durable or useful.

Independent execution means a different agent successfully invoked a registered service. Acceptance is that consumer’s judgment against a stated criterion. Neither is an independent human evaluation. Root activation requires matching validation, activation and state-preservation evidence.

How counts were reconciled

The 35 campaign peer executions are a subset of the fresh run’s 42. The 27 campaign app identities are not added to the fresh run’s 32. Per-window app counts may repeat identities. Imported history is excluded.

A correction to earlier informal updates: a later Promise Gate acceptance concerned an invocation from the previous run. It is excluded from fresh-run adoption. Fresh V4 has four independently executed apps and three with accepted use; the campaign has three of each.

Judgments and executions need not have equal totals: criteria differ, judgments can arrive later, and rejections can concern failed or unverifiable execution. Older smaller app counts used narrower registry/snapshot scopes; the publication count here includes all distinct identities with exact authenticated receipts.

Evidence sources and what was not re-tested

Reviewed sources include model/controller journals, scoped native receipts, publication and invocation records, consumer judgments, root validation/activation receipts, source/test output, goal logs, operational timelines and four canonical budget ledgers.

The audit did not rerun every endpoint, inspect other owners’ resources, reconcile provider invoices, verify model weights or obtain independent human value ratings. Agent claims were leads to investigate, not proof by themselves.

What the design cannot establish causally

Population, prompts, models, context, tools and reliability changed together. The 30/20 context split was not randomized and prior exposure differed. Raw counts cannot rank models or attribute results to personality.

All participants belonged to one owner-controlled experiment. Human willingness to connect strangers’ agents, durable demand, economic exchange and a collective advantage over isolated agents remain untested.

What is excluded from the shareable report

Original transcripts, credentials, owner identifiers, internal chat/sandbox addresses and raw operational logs are excluded. The website contains curated descriptions and aggregate evidence.
